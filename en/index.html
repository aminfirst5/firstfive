02 First service

The AI coach supports young people with questions arising at the start of working life.

The AI coach is the first First Five Years service. Alongside it, we are building aggregated phenomenon data to support decision-making.

03 First pilot

Young people used the AI coach. The same situations began to recur across conversations.

Young people sought support with direction, strengths, skills development, job search and difficult situations at work.

Ten recurring patterns were identified across different users. The pilot showed that individual support can also create shared understanding.

04 Identifying a phenomenon

An observation becomes a signal only when it withstands comparison.

An individual conversation belongs to the user. On its own, it is not a basis for conclusions about a person, service or workplace.

A signal worth examining emerges when the same pattern appears across several separate conversations. It may point to job search, gaps in services, management or a transition into working life.

A signal is treated as a grounded question for services, employers and researchers to investigate further.

05 Decision-making

Decisions should be possible before a problem appears in the outcome.

Labour market statistics tell us what happened. Recurring signals may help us understand why.

First Five Years is exploring whether aggregated phenomenon-level insight could complement existing statistics and feedback, revealing where a service, management practice or transition fails to carry.

One pilot is not a basis for national conclusions. We are building and testing a method for assessing the reliability of these signals.

For decision-makers, the value is an earlier view: a stronger basis for directing pilots, research and resources to the places where attachment to working life is weakening.

08 Principles

The work is grounded in transparency, voluntary participation and user protection.

01

Users know they are interacting with AI

Users are told clearly that the AI coach is not a person.

02

The service does not assess or classify people

No scores, personality classifications or employment predictions.

03

Phenomenon-level insight is separated from individual users

The shared view is based on aggregated observations, not published conversations.

04

Data use is based on voluntary participation

Users can withdraw from data use without losing access to the AI coach.

05

Conversation data is not commercialised

It is not sold or shared for profiling by third parties.
