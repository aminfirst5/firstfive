Young people should not have to navigate their first years of working life alone.

The start of working life is a phase lasting several years. Direction, capability and a sense of belonging are built during this time. Support should be easy to access precisely then.

Value for the young person as the primary objective

First Five Years brings an AI coach into situations where a young person is considering their direction, strengths, job search or a difficult situation at work.

The coach is AI rather than a person, and its value belongs first to the user: a chance to pause, make sense of the situation and identify a next step when the question is current.

The societal relevance of recurring experiences

When the same situation emerges across several separate conversations, it may point to a phenomenon larger than any one individual.

A signal may relate to job search, services, management, workplace practices or transitions from education and services into work. The subject of analysis is the phenomenon, not the person.

An earlier view for decision-making

Labour market statistics are essential for understanding outcomes. On their own, they do not show where direction was lost, job search stalled or a first workplace began to fail.

We are exploring whether aggregated phenomenon-level insight can complement the current picture and help direct pilots, research and resources earlier. The method must be demonstrated step by step, with larger user groups and independent evaluation.

Data protection and the position of the user

People are not scored, classified or predicted. Individual conversations are not published, and conversation data is not sold.

Users know they are interacting with AI. They can withdraw from data use without losing access to the AI coach.

Building a model for collaboration

First Five Years invites young people, employers, the public sector, researchers, funders and organisations working with young people to join us. Different perspectives are necessary to build both individual support and shared insight responsibly.

Our mission is to make the first years of working life a phase where support arrives in time and recurring barriers become visible before they turn into permanent outcomes.
